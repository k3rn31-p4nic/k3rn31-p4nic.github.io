Hunter

A private, self-hosted signal inbox — for one person.

Hunter is a personal, single-user tool that collects research papers, research programmes, campus notices, remote jobs, and other technical opportunities from public feeds, ranks them against the owner's own resume and preferences, and presents them as a single prioritized feed. It runs as one self-hosted Go binary, reachable only over the owner's private Tailscale network — there is no public sign-up, no multi-tenant access, and no second account can use this installation.

What it connects to

Hunter optionally connects to the owner's own Google Account (Gmail and Calendar) to surface application-related emails and create deadline reminders. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is accessed and why.

Who runs it

This is a personal, non-commercial project built and operated by a single individual for their own use. It is not a public product or service.
