Privacy Policy

Applies to: Hunter (personal, single-user application) · Last updated: 2026-09-24

Hunter is a personal, non-commercial, self-hosted application built and used by a single individual (the "owner"). It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and no one other than the owner can connect an account to this installation. This policy explains what data Hunter accesses from Google, why, and how it is handled.

1. What Google data Hunter accesses

Hunter requests the following Google OAuth scopes, only from the owner's own Google Account(s):

ScopePurpose
gmail.readonly Reads new incoming Gmail messages only, starting from the connection point forward. Hunter never lists historical mail and never modifies, labels, or deletes anything in the mailbox. Used to surface campus notices and to detect and classify application-status emails (applied / interview / offer / rejected, etc.).
gmail.send Sends email as the owner for two purposes only: (a) a daily/urgent digest of new opportunities to the owner's own inbox, and (b) drafting job applications the owner has explicitly reviewed and approved. This scope cannot read mail beyond what gmail.readonly already permits.
calendar.events Creates calendar events (e.g. application deadlines) on the owner's primary calendar. Limited to managing events Hunter itself creates — not general calendar read/write access.
openid, email Identifies which Google account has been connected.

2. How the data is used

Data pulled from Gmail is used only to rank and surface opportunities, track application status, and generate reminders — all for the owner's own benefit. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with any third party for their own purposes.

A small amount of message or job-listing text may be sent to AI language model providers (Google's Gemini API, and a separate OpenAI-compatible completion endpoint) strictly to classify an application email's pipeline stage or to judge a job listing's fit against the owner's resume. These providers process the text to return a classification and do not receive standing access to the Google account.

3. Storage and security

  • Hunter runs as a single self-hosted process, reachable only over the owner's private Tailscale network — it is never exposed on the public internet.
  • Google refresh tokens are encrypted at rest (AES) with a locally held master key before being stored.
  • No Google data is stored in shared, third-party, or cloud databases outside the owner's own server.

4. Data retention and deletion

Fetched item and application data is retained only as long as the owner keeps this installation running. The owner can disconnect Google access and delete all locally stored data at any time by revoking Hunter's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions and removing the local database.

5. Limited Use disclosure

Hunter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
